Folders

Checked folders get cleaned and sorted. Unchecked ones stay exactly as they are.

How selection and the per-folder mode work

Clicking a folder applies the selection to all of its subfolders — you can set individual ones differently afterwards.

The dropdown on the right of each folder sets what duplicates are compared against in that folder, direct entries only, no subfolders. The default for all folders lives under Duplicates in the options.

Click the tag on a duplicate to keep that specific copy anyway.

Select & move adds a second, amber checkbox to every row. Tick as many bookmarks or folders as you like and move them together — a folder always takes its contents with it.
