MetaClean Pro Version 4 — Metadata & Forensics Workbench 100% local

GitHub · NoAuthZone/MetaClean-Pro

Inspect and normalize EXIF/TIFF, GPS, XMP, IPTC-IIM, PNG text, WebP/RIFF and ISO-BMFF / QuickTime metadata directly in your browser. The Forensics view adds magic-byte verification, hex headers, entropy sampling, structural segment maps, ASCII/Unicode strings, trailing-payload and polyglot candidates, plus a transparent heuristic risk score. JPEG/PNG/WebP can also receive edited XMP metadata in a new copy. Nothing is uploaded.

ExifTool-like does not mean full ExifTool compatibility: proprietary MakerNote decoding, many RAW/vendor formats, full C2PA/JUMBF interpretation and safe in-place writing for complex containers are intentionally outside this browser-native build. The forensic score is a triage heuristic, not an antivirus or malware verdict.
